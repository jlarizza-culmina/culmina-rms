# Caffè Correcto — Full Drink Recipes

---

## CAFFÈ

### 1. Espresso
**Glassware:** Demitasse cup (2–3 oz)
**Ingredients:** 7–9g finely ground espresso
**Instructions:** Pull a single shot at 9 bars pressure, 25–30 seconds extraction, yield 1 oz. Serve immediately with a small spoon and a glass of still water.
**Garnish:** None. Small spoon on the saucer.

---

### 2. Doppio
**Glassware:** Demitasse cup (3–4 oz)
**Ingredients:** 14–18g finely ground espresso
**Instructions:** Pull a double shot at 9 bars pressure, 25–30 seconds extraction, yield 2 oz. Serve immediately.
**Garnish:** None.

---

### 3. Americano
**Glassware:** 6 oz ceramic cup
**Ingredients:** 1 oz espresso (double shot), 4 oz hot water
**Instructions:** Pull double espresso shot. Add hot water to cup first, then pour espresso over. Preserves crema.
**Garnish:** None. Serve with milk on the side if requested.

---

### 4. Cappuccino
**Glassware:** 5–6 oz ceramic cappuccino cup
**Ingredients:** 1 oz espresso, 2 oz steamed milk, 1 oz thick milk foam
**Instructions:** Pull single espresso shot into cup. Steam milk to 140°F, creating thick microfoam. Pour steamed milk over espresso, holding back foam with a spoon, then spoon foam on top. Ratio: 1/3 espresso, 1/3 steamed milk, 1/3 foam.
**Garnish:** Optional light dusting of cocoa powder. Never cinnamon.

---

### 5. Latte Macchiato
**Glassware:** Tall glass (8–10 oz)
**Ingredients:** 2 oz espresso, 5 oz steamed milk, 1 oz foam
**Instructions:** Steam milk to 140°F with light foam. Pour steamed milk into tall glass first. Pull double espresso shot and pour slowly through the foam — the espresso layers through the milk creating three visible layers. Do not stir.
**Garnish:** None. The layering is the visual.

---

### 6. Flat White
**Glassware:** 5 oz ceramic cup
**Ingredients:** 1.5 oz espresso (ristretto double), 3 oz steamed milk
**Instructions:** Pull a ristretto double shot (shorter, more concentrated). Steam milk to 130°F with very fine, velvety microfoam — no thick foam. Pour milk in a steady stream over espresso. The result is silky, dense, stronger than a cappuccino.
**Garnish:** Optional simple latte art.

---

### 7. Shakerato
**Glassware:** Chilled coupe or martini glass (4–5 oz)
**Ingredients:** 1.5 oz espresso (hot), 0.5 oz simple syrup, ice for shaking
**Instructions:** Pull double espresso shot. Add to cocktail shaker with simple syrup and a generous amount of ice. Shake hard for 15 seconds until very frothy and cold. Double strain into chilled coupe. The shake creates a thick, cold espresso foam.
**Garnish:** None. The foam is the garnish.

---

### 8. Caffè Freddo
**Glassware:** Small rocks glass or 4 oz tumbler
**Ingredients:** 1.5 oz espresso, 0.5 oz simple syrup, ice
**Instructions:** Pull double espresso shot, stir in simple syrup while hot. Chill over ice or refrigerate. Serve over ice in a small glass. Can be prepared in batch and held refrigerated.
**Garnish:** None.

---

### 9. Bicerin
**Glassware:** Small cylindrical glass (4–5 oz) — traditionally a short wide glass
**Ingredients:** 1 oz espresso, 1.5 oz drinking chocolate (thick, hot), 1 oz heavy cream (lightly whipped)
**Instructions:** Warm the glass. Pour hot drinking chocolate into the bottom. Pull espresso shot and pour gently over the chocolate — do not stir, let it layer. Float lightly whipped cream on top by pouring over the back of a spoon. Serve without a straw — drink through the layers.
**Garnish:** None. Do not stir. The guest drinks through three layers.

---

### 10. Cioccolato Caldo
**Glassware:** 6 oz ceramic cup or small mug
**Ingredients:** 6 oz whole milk, 1 oz dark chocolate (70%), 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp cornstarch
**Instructions:** Heat milk in a small saucepan. Whisk in finely chopped chocolate, sugar, and cornstarch. Stir continuously over medium heat until thick and glossy, 3–4 minutes. Pour into cup.
**Garnish:** Optional small dollop of whipped cream. A curl of orange zest is elegant.

---

### 11. Bianco e Nero
**Glassware:** 6 oz rocks glass or dessert glass
**Ingredients:** 1 scoop vanilla gelato, 1 oz espresso (hot)
**Instructions:** Place scoop of vanilla gelato in the glass. Pull single espresso shot and pour directly over the gelato. Serve immediately with a long spoon.
**Garnish:** None. Serve immediately before gelato melts.

## CAFFÈ CORRETTO

### 12. Il Corretto
**Glassware:** Demitasse cup with a small shot glass on the side
**Ingredients:** 1 oz espresso, 0.5–1 oz of guest's choice: grappa, sambuca, or amaro
**Instructions:** Pull single espresso shot. Serve the spirit in a small shot glass alongside. Guest adds as much or as little as they prefer. Alternatively, pour spirit directly into the espresso for a traditional single-vessel preparation.
**Garnish:** None. The ritual is the presentation.

## NEGRONI

### 13. Classico
**Glassware:** Rocks glass (8 oz)
**Ingredients:** 1 oz Campari, 1 oz gin, 1 oz vermouth rosso
**Instructions:** Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice. Stir 30 rotations until well chilled and properly diluted. Strain over one large ice cube in rocks glass.
**Garnish:** Orange peel — express oils over the glass, run around the rim, place on the drink.

---

### 14. Sbagliato
**Glassware:** Wine glass or rocks glass (8 oz)
**Ingredients:** 1 oz Campari, 1 oz vermouth rosso, 2 oz Prosecco
**Instructions:** Add Campari and vermouth rosso to glass over ice. Top with chilled Prosecco. Stir gently once — do not over-stir or you lose the bubbles.
**Garnish:** Orange slice or orange peel.

## CLASSICI

### 28. Old Fashioned
**Glassware:** Rocks glass (8–10 oz)
**Ingredients:** 2 oz bourbon, 0.25 oz simple syrup (or 1 sugar cube), 2 dashes Angostura bitters, 1 dash orange bitters
**Instructions:** Add sugar and bitters to glass. Add a splash of water and muddle until sugar is dissolved. Add bourbon and one large ice cube. Stir gently 10–15 rotations.
**Garnish:** Orange peel — express oils over the glass, place on the drink. Optional brandied cherry.

---

### 29. Paper Plane
**Glassware:** Coupe (5–6 oz), chilled
**Ingredients:** 0.75 oz bourbon, 0.75 oz Aperol, 0.75 oz Amaro Nonino, 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice
**Instructions:** Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice. Shake hard 12–15 seconds. Double strain into chilled coupe.
**Garnish:** None, or a thin lemon wheel on the rim.

---

### 30. Vodka Tonic
**Glassware:** Highball glass (10–12 oz)
**Ingredients:** 2 oz vodka, 4 oz premium tonic water, ice
**Instructions:** Fill glass with ice. Add vodka. Top with chilled tonic. Stir gently once.
**Garnish:** Lime wedge.

---

### 31. Gin & Tonic
**Glassware:** Copa glass or highball (10–12 oz)
**Ingredients:** 2 oz gin, 4 oz premium tonic water, ice
**Instructions:** Fill glass with ice. Add gin. Top with chilled tonic. Stir gently once.
**Garnish:** Lime wedge. Optional: cucumber slice, juniper berries, or rosemary sprig depending on gin expression.

---

### 32. Margarita
**Glassware:** Rocks glass (8–10 oz) or coupe
**Ingredients:** 2 oz tequila (blanco), 1 oz Cointreau, 0.75 oz fresh lime juice, 0.25 oz simple syrup
**Instructions:** Rim glass with salt (half-rim only). Combine all ingredients in shaker with ice. Shake hard 12 seconds. Strain over fresh ice (rocks) or straight up (coupe).
**Garnish:** Lime wheel on the rim.

---

### 33. Whiskey Sour
**Glassware:** Rocks glass (8 oz) or coupe
**Ingredients:** 2 oz bourbon, 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice, 0.5 oz simple syrup, 1 egg white (optional)
**Instructions:** If using egg white: dry shake all ingredients without ice 10 seconds, then add ice and shake hard 15 seconds. If no egg white: shake with ice 12 seconds. Strain over fresh ice (rocks) or into chilled coupe.
**Garnish:** Lemon wheel and 2–3 drops Angostura bitters on the foam.

---

### 34. Moscow Mule
**Glassware:** Copper mug (12 oz)
**Ingredients:** 2 oz vodka, 0.5 oz fresh lime juice, 4 oz ginger beer, ice
**Instructions:** Fill copper mug with ice. Add vodka and lime juice. Top with ginger beer. Stir gently once.
**Garnish:** Lime wheel and fresh mint sprig.

---

### 35. Mojito
**Glassware:** Highball glass (12 oz)
**Ingredients:** 2 oz white rum, 0.75 oz fresh lime juice, 0.5 oz simple syrup, 8–10 fresh mint leaves, 2 oz soda water, ice
**Instructions:** Add mint leaves and simple syrup to glass. Press mint gently with a muddler — do not shred. Add lime juice and rum. Fill with crushed ice. Top with soda. Stir once with a bar spoon to lift the mint.
**Garnish:** Fresh mint bouquet and a lime wheel.
